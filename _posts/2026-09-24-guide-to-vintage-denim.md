## The Thrill of the Hunt

Finding a perfect pair of vintage Levi's 501s or a classic RRL jacket is one of the most rewarding experiences in reselling. It’s not just about the profit; it’s about preserving a piece of history.

### What to Look For
When you're out sourcing, always keep an eye out for these key indicators of quality and age:
* **The "Big E" Tab:** On Levi's, a capital 'E' on the red tab usually means the garment was made before 1971.
* **Selvedge Edges:** Roll up the cuff. If you see a clean, finished edge with a colored thread (often red), you've found selvedge denim, which is highly sought after.
* **Single Stitch Hems:** Older garments often feature single-stitch construction on the hems rather than double-stitch.

Happy hunting!
