It's tempting to buy massive, decommissioned enterprise servers off eBay for cheap. They have dozens of cores and terabytes of RAM. But there's a hidden cost: **Electricity.**

Running old enterprise gear 24/7 can add hundreds of dollars to your annual power bill. 

### The TinyMiniMicro Revolution
Instead of enterprise racks, I've shifted my focus to 1-liter mini PCs and low-power hardware. Modern processors are incredibly efficient. A cluster of mini PCs drawing 15 watts each can often outperform a 5-year-old enterprise server drawing 200 watts at idle. 

By carefully selecting hardware with low idle power draw and optimizing my BIOS settings, my entire homelab sips electricity while providing more than enough compute power for my containers, automation scripts, and media streaming.
