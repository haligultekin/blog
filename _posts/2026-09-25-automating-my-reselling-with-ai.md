## Working Smarter, Not Harder

Running an eBay store takes a lot of manual labor—taking photos, writing SEO titles, measuring garments, and managing listings. I decided to change that by building an AI-powered homelab server.

### The Pipeline
Now, my workflow is entirely automated using Telegram and an AI agent:
1. I snap a photo of a garment and send it to my Telegram bot.
2. The AI Vision model analyzes the tags, brand, and fabric.
3. The system drafts an SEO-optimized title and generates a local draft.
4. It's instantly ready to publish via the eBay API!

It has completely revolutionized how much volume I can process in a single day.
