One of the most popular gateway drugs into the homelab hobby is building a personal media server. The ability to host, organize, and stream your own video and audio files anywhere in the world is incredibly rewarding.

### Hardware Acceleration is Everything
When setting up a media server, CPU power isn't everything. The secret to smooth streaming, especially when transcoding high-bitrate video to a mobile device, is Hardware Acceleration. By passing through an integrated GPU (like Intel QuickSync) or a dedicated low-profile graphics card to the media container, the server can handle multiple simultaneous streams without breaking a sweat or consuming massive amounts of power.

It has completely replaced traditional streaming services in our household, giving us total ownership over our media library.
