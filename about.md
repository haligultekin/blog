# About Me

Hi! Welcome to my digital space.

I am passionate about the intersection of classic, tangible history and cutting-edge technology. On a daily basis, I manage a **vintage and heritage clothing reselling studio**—specializing in iconic brands like Levi's, RRL, Iron Heart, Lee, and Wrangler. I spend my time sourcing, measuring, and curating pieces that carry incredible history (from selvedge denim to single-stitch vintage shirts).

But behind the scenes, my entire workflow is powered by a custom-built **Homelab Server**. I am deeply interested in network infrastructure, self-hosting, and Artificial Intelligence. 

Instead of doing things manually, I build automated pipelines:
* I run Docker container orchestration to manage my services.
* I self-host local AI LLMs and Vision models to analyze garment photos and generate SEO titles.
* I utilize Telegram bots to automate my eBay drafting process straight from my phone.

This blog is where I document my homelab architecture, automation scripts, and vintage denim finds. Whether you're a tech enthusiast looking for Docker strategies or a reseller hunting for Big E Levi's, I hope you find something inspiring here!
